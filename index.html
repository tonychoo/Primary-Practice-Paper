How it works

Your child answers on screen. Most questions mark themselves when the paper is finished. For sentence answers, a model answer appears and a parent decides whether to give the mark.

Use "New attempt" to start again or to let another child try. Each attempt keeps its own answers and score on this device.

Free practice for Singapore Primary 3 pupils. Original material written for home revision. Not affiliated with MOE, SEAB or any school.
No sign-up, no cookies, no tracking. Answers stay in your own browser. Privacy
