Privacy

Last updated 13 October 2026

What we collect

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It does not send your answers or your child's name anywhere.

What is saved on your device

When your child does a paper, the name typed in and the answers are saved in your browser's local storage on this device only. We cannot see this data.

You can remove it at any time with “Delete this attempt” or “Clear all saved attempts” on the paper page, or by clearing your browser's site data.

Backups

“Back up attempts” saves a file to your own device. Restoring reads that file inside your browser. The file is never uploaded.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep technical logs such as IP addresses to run and protect its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If advertising or a visitor counter is added in future, this page will be updated first to explain what it collects.
